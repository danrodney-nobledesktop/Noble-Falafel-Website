Fried while you watch

Nothing sits under a heat lamp. When you order, we shape the falafel from chickpeas we soaked overnight and drop them in the oil. About four minutes later they're in your pita, still crackling.

It's how falafel has been sold on streets from Aleppo to the Lower East Side for a long time. We just kept the good part.

Bring the cart to your party

Office lunches, birthdays, block parties and weddings. We roll up with the fryer, or drop off trays ready to share. Fill in the coupon and it opens an email to us.

A cart, a fryer, fair prices

Good food shouldn't cost a fortune

So it doesn't. A cart has no dining room to pay for, and we'd rather spend on chickpeas, tahini and fresh herbs than on rent. That's how the classic pita stays at $7.

Why we close on Mondays

Monday is for shopping: dried chickpeas by the sack, parsley by the crate, a new tub of tahini. It all gets soaked and chopped so Tuesday's first batch is as good as Sunday's last.
